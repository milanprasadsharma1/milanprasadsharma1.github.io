Professional Profile

My name is Milan Prasad Sharma, an Information Technology professional and educator with a strong interest in digital transformation, technology-enabled public services and knowledge sharing. I am passionate about using technology to create practical solutions that improve efficiency, accessibility and the quality of everyday work.

I have completed a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Information Technology (BSc CSIT), which provided me with a strong foundation in computer science, software development, networking, databases, information systems and modern information technology.

With more than three years of professional experience as an IT Officer, I have been involved in IT infrastructure, system and network support, troubleshooting, digital services, electronic office practices, document digitization, data backup and technology-related coordination.

My professional interests include website development, IT support, networking, system administration, e-governance, digital documentation, cybersecurity awareness and IT training. I enjoy developing practical web solutions and helping organizations make effective use of digital technologies.

Alongside my IT career, I have more than 10 years of teaching experience. Teaching has strengthened my communication, mentoring and presentation skills and has given me the opportunity to simplify technical concepts and make learning more practical and engaging.

I believe technology is most valuable when it solves real problems and creates meaningful improvements. My goal is to continue growing as an IT professional and educator while contributing to meaningful digital transformation and sharing practical knowledge with others.
